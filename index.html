The intensive method

Five steps, in this order, every time.

Skip any one of them and the problem comes back. This is why our visits take longer than a spray route.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    We walk the property with a flashlight and a checklist: kitchen, pantry, voids, basement, attic, garage, and the exterior perimeter. We look for the pest, its food, its water, and its route in.

  2. 02

    Identify

    Species and life stage, confirmed. A bait that works on one ant species fails on another, and a mouse is not a rat. Identification decides the product, the placement, and the follow-up interval.

  3. 03

    Treat

    Targeted application of Ontario-registered products, placed where pests travel and away from food contact surfaces. We use the least product that will do the job, then tell you exactly what was used and where.

  4. 04

    Exclude

    We seal what let them in: gaps around pipes, weep vents, door sweeps, foundation cracks, soffit openings. Exclusion is the step most companies skip, and it is the reason the problem keeps coming back.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Monitors and follow-up visits confirm the population is falling. If the target pest is still active between scheduled visits, we return and re-treat at no charge.

How much does a pest control visit cost?

The inspection is free. After it you receive a written quote with the treatment plan and the price before any work starts. No surprises on the invoice.

Are the products safe for children and pets?

We use products registered in Ontario and apply them only where the target pest travels. Your technician tells you what was used, where, and how long to wait before the area is used again.

Do I need to leave my home during treatment?

For most treatments, no. For fogging, bed bug heat treatment, or heavy cockroach work we tell you the exact window to stay out.

What is your guarantee?

If the target pest is still active between scheduled visits, we return and re-treat at no charge for as long as you are on an active plan.

Do you service restaurants and food plants?

Yes. We run scheduled IPM programmes with monitoring, trend reports, and documentation formatted for public health inspections.

How fast can you come out?

Same day or next day for most of Windsor and Essex County. Commercial clients have a 24-hour line.
