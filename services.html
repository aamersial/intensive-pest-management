Residential

Inside the house, not just around it.

Most home treatments cover the exterior perimeter. Ours also covers the rooms where pests actually live: kitchen, pantry, basement, attic, and garage.

CockroachesTwo visits

German and Oriental cockroaches

Cockroaches breed in voids, behind cabinets, and around warm motors. Surface spray reaches none of that, so we bait and dust the harbourage instead, then verify with monitors.

  • Gel bait and void dusting where they nest
  • Growth regulator when the population is established
  • Second visit at 14 days to confirm the knockdown
RodentsExclusion included

Mice and rats

Trapping removes the animals you have. Exclusion stops the next ones. We do both in the same visit, and we map the entry points on the report so you can see where they were getting in.

  • Attic, crawlspace, and foundation inspection
  • Snap and multi-catch traps placed out of reach of pets
  • Entry points sealed with rodent-grade materials
Bed bugsDiscreet

Bed bugs

Unmarked vehicles and plain equipment on request. We confirm the species before treatment, because bat bugs and carpet beetles get misidentified constantly.

  • Room by room inspection of seams, frames, and outlets
  • Heat treatment plus targeted residual where heat cannot reach
  • Two follow-up inspections before the file is closed
AntsSpecies matched bait

Ants

Carpenter ants, pavement ants, and pharaoh ants do not respond to the same product. We identify the species first, then bait accordingly. Spraying a trail makes pharaoh ants split into new colonies.

  • Trail mapping and nest location
  • Non-repellent bait matched to the species
  • Exterior perimeter treatment where ants are entering
TermitesMonitoring

Termites and wood destroying insects

Termite work is a monitoring job, not a one time spray. We inspect for mud tubes and galleries, correct the moisture that attracts them, and install stations to track activity over time.

  • Moisture and gallery inspection of sills, joists, and framing
  • Bait monitoring stations with scheduled checks
  • Written findings suitable for a real estate transaction
Wasps and hornetsSame day where possible

Wasps, hornets, and bees

Nests at eaves, soffits, sheds, and play structures are removed at dusk when the colony is inside. Honey bee swarms are referred to a local beekeeper instead of treated.

  • Nest removal and treatment of the attachment site
  • Sealing of the gap that allowed the nest
  • Follow-up check at 10 days in summer
Commercial

Documentation that stands up to an inspection.

Public health inspectors do not ask whether pests were found. They ask what you did about it, when, and who signed off. Our programmes are built to answer that with records, not assurances.

  • Monitoring stations mapped and numbered across the site
  • Trend reports that show activity by location and month
  • Corrective action log for sanitation and structural findings
  • Service records signed by a licensed applicator
  • 24-hour line for urgent findings and inspection day support

What we do about each problem.
